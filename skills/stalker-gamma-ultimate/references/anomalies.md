# Anomalies and Artifacts

Anomalies are pockets of warped physics scattered across the Zone. They are
invisible or nearly so, instantly lethal, and the source of artifacts — the
Zone's most valuable trade good. Every stalker learns this rule first: never
walk in a straight line through unknown ground.

## Detecting anomalies

### Bolts
The cheapest detector ever made. Throw a bolt ahead of you; if it lands
normally the ground is safe, if it triggers a discharge or veers off, an
anomaly is there. Every stalker carries an effectively infinite supply.

### Detectors
Handheld detectors, up to the scientific Veles and Svarog models, beep
faster as you approach an anomaly or artifact. Advanced models can pinpoint
artifacts that lesser detectors miss. Keep one equipped in anomaly fields.

## Thermal anomalies

### Burner
A column of superheated air that erupts to extreme temperatures when
triggered. Looks like heat haze when calm. Produces the Droplets, Fireball,
and Crystal artifacts.

### Comet
A roaming thermal phenomenon drifting along a set path. Do not stand in its
lane; it will find you.

## Electro anomalies

### Electro
Crackling arcs of electricity hanging in the air. Triggers on proximity with
a lethal discharge. Yields Battery, Shell, Snowflake, Sparkler, Flash, and
Moonlight artifacts.

### Tesla
A stronger, wider electro field. Treat it like an electro with a much bigger
radius and no mercy.

## Chemical anomalies

### Burnt Fuzz
Moss-like growths hanging from trees and structures. Mild on a brush in the
south, far more dangerous in the north — a touch there can take a quarter of
your health through good armor. Produces Thorn, Crystal Thorn, and Urchin
artifacts.

### Fruit Punch
Pools of glowing green acid. Instant, horrific damage. Never wade through
standing liquid in the Zone.

### Gas
Clouds of toxic vapor, often in lowlands and basements. A gas mask with
working filters is mandatory; the cloud drifts, so watch the wind and do not
linger.

## Gravitational anomalies

### Springboard
Launches whatever triggers it violently into the air. Survivable with good
armor, but the landing rarely is.

### Whirligig
A spinning vortex that shreds anything caught inside. Listen for the rising
whine and give it a wide berth.

### Vortex
The deadliest common gravitational anomaly. It drags victims within ten to
fifteen meters into its center, lifts them, and tears them apart with an
energy discharge. Produces Wrenched, Night Star, Gravi, and Goldfish
artifacts.

### Lift
Throws the victim upward rather than inward. Less lethal than a vortex, but
it still breaks bones and armor.

## Other phenomena

### Psi-anomalies
Zones of psychic pressure that drain sanity and health. Psy-protection is
the only defense — without it, turn around.

### Teleport and space anomalies
Rare spatial warps that relocate whatever enters them. Some are one-way.
Mark them and move on.

### Emissions (blowouts)
Periodic psychic storms that sweep the entire Zone. When the sirens sound,
take cover indoors or underground immediately — caught outside, a stalker
dies. Emissions also shuffle anomaly positions and seed fresh artifacts.

## Artifact hunting basics

- Artifacts spawn in and around anomaly fields, especially after emissions.
- Never grab an artifact without checking its stats — many are radioactive
  or harmful until combined with others on a belt.
- Artifacts are the Zone's best currency: traders pay well, and the
  scientists pay best of all.
- Anomalies chew through suits fast; armor up to enter a field, and expect
  a repair bill after.
