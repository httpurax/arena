# Artifacts

Artifacts are the Zone's strangest treasure: objects formed inside
anomaly fields that grant powerful bonuses — always paired with
drawbacks. A good artifact set can define an entire playstyle.

## What artifacts are

- Artifacts spawn in and around anomaly fields, where the Zone's warped
  physics concentrates into solid form.
- Every artifact has positive properties (health regeneration, carry
  weight, protection) balanced by negative ones — most commonly added
  radiation exposure.
- Rarer artifacts have stronger effects; common ones give small but
  useful edges.

## Anomalies

- Anomalies are pockets of dangerous warped physics: gravitational
  (Springboard, Vortex, Whirligig), thermal (Burner), electrical, and
  chemical/acid varieties.
- Most are hard to see until triggered. **Bolts** — which you carry in
  infinite supply — are thrown ahead to trigger and reveal them safely.
- Never walk blind into a field: sweep with bolts, watch the discharge,
  then move.

## Detectors

- **Echo**: the basic detector. Beeps faster as you near an artifact but
  has a limited database — it misses many artifact types.
- **Bear**: adds direction and distance indication on top of the audio
  signal; still a limited database.
- **Veles**: next-generation scanner. Detects radiation as well as
  artifacts, triangulates positions, and recognizes the full known
  artifact database.
- **Svarog**: prototype detector that reveals previously unstudied
  artifacts and anomalies. The endgame tool for serious hunters.
- Better detectors find rarer artifacts — upgrading the detector is
  upgrading your income.

## Hunting technique

- Equip the detector and sweep anomaly fields slowly, following the
  beeping to the artifact's exact spot.
- Artifacts are often invisible until you are nearly on top of them —
  trust the detector, not your eyes.
- Grab the artifact and retreat immediately; lingering in the field
  invites a discharge to the face.
- Heat protection (from suit or artifacts) matters: some fields punish
  greed with lethal damage.

## Containers and radiation

- Artifacts irradiate their carrier. They must be kept in containers on
  the belt — never loose in the pack.
- Lead-lined containers reduce the radiation that reaches you; standard
  containers protect less.
- Suits provide a limited number of artifact slots, and technician
  upgrades can add more containers. Plan your suit around your artifact
  set.
- Balance the books: stack too many hot artifacts and the radiation
  outweighs the bonuses — offset with anti-rad artifacts or protection.

## Emissions and respawns

- After an emission (blowout), artifacts respawn across anomaly fields,
  restocking hunting grounds.
- Post-emission is prime hunting time — but every other hunter in the
  Zone knows it too.

## Trading

- Traders buy artifacts, and rare specimens fetch high prices.
- Scientists and certain NPCs pay premiums or request specific
  artifacts for their research — check job boards before selling a rare
  find to a regular trader.
- Some artifacts are quest items: do not sell the one an NPC asked for.

## Tips

- Your first serious money often comes from artifacts — invest early
  earnings in a better detector.
- A Veles pays for itself quickly in found rarities.
- Always carry anti-radiation meds when hunting; the field and the loot
  both irradiate you.
