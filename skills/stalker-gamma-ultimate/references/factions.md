# Factions of the Zone

The Zone is carved up by armed groups with incompatible visions of what it
is and who it belongs to. Your faction choice decides where you start, who
trades with you, who shoots on sight, and which storylines open up.

## Loners (Free stalkers)

Unaffiliated stalkers chasing artifacts, money, or answers on their own
terms. The default choice and the most flexible: no faction hates you at
the start, and most storylines are open. If in doubt, start here.

## Duty

Ex-military hardliners who believe the Zone is a wound on the world that
must be contained and destroyed. Disciplined, heavily armed, and utterly
hostile to Freedom. Their traders stock excellent armor and rifles. If you
like order and firepower, Duty is home.

## Freedom

Anarchists and idealists who believe the Zone belongs to everyone and its
secrets must be free. Locked in an ideological and very literal war with
Duty. Relaxed, well-connected, and fond of light, mobile gear.

## Bandits

Criminals who prey on stalkers for profit. Everyone's enemy except the few
who pay them. Joining them means easy early money and universal distrust.

## Mercenaries

Professionals who fight for whoever pays. Competent, well-equipped, and
universally suspected of working for shadowy outside interests. Good pay,
few friends.

## Military

The Ukrainian army's Zone contingent, holding the perimeter and key
installations. Powerful but stretched thin. Few stalkers start here, and
fewer are welcome in stalker camps while wearing the uniform.

## Monolith

Fanatical cultists guarding the Center of the Zone, devoted to the
monolithic artifact at its heart. They do not negotiate, they do not
retreat, and they shoot everyone else on sight. Not a starting faction —
an endgame enemy.

## Clear Sky

Researchers and scouts obsessed with understanding the Zone's emissions and
anomalies. Small, scholarly, and based in the Great Swamps. Excellent
contacts for anomaly science and the Last Dawn storyline.

## Ecologists (Scientists)

The scientific expedition studying the Zone under military protection. They
pay the best prices for artifacts and mutant specimens in the entire Zone.
Befriend them if you plan to hunt artifacts for a living.

## Renegades

Outcasts and deserters from every faction, squatting in the Swamps. Hostile
to nearly everyone, including others of their own kind on a bad day.

## Sin

A secretive, sinister faction operating from the shadows with unknown
masters and unknown goals. They are the antagonists of the Mortal Sin
storyline. Encountering them means you have wandered somewhere important.

## Faction relations that matter

- **Duty vs Freedom:** the Zone's oldest blood feud. Helping one angers the
  other. There is no middle ground past a point.
- **Everyone vs Monolith:** no faction parleys with the cult.
- **Bandits vs Loners:** bandits rob loners by trade; loners return the favor
  with buckshot.
- **Ecologists** are protected by the Military and tolerated by almost
  everyone — shooting scientists closes a lot of doors.

## Joining and goodwill

- Do jobs for a faction to raise goodwill: better prices, better stock,
  access to their technicians and guides.
- Attacking a faction's members tanks your standing fast and closes their
  storylines.
- You can switch allegiances, but old enemies remember.
