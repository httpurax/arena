# Where should I build my base in ATM10?

**Short answer (judgement, not a quest requirement):** Start with a main base in the **Overworld**. Keep room to expand; use the **Mining Dimension** as a separate mining/quarry site once you reach its quest, and consider **Compact Machines** later if you want an enclosed build. The pack does **not** prescribe a best biome, coordinates, base footprint, or a mandatory home dimension. This is a practical choice, not a verified quest objective.

## The choices

| Spot | What the pack actually says | Recommendation (*judgement*) |
|---|---|---|
| **Overworld main base** | In Chapter 2: Allthemodium, quest **“Overworld”** (`2C4299D33D419AA0`) says placing the Teleport Pad in the Overworld goes to the Mining Dimension; **“Teleport Pad”** (`07FDA46D83F8360D`) describes entering other dimensions. The **“Claiming Chunks”** task (`103C42C743E2A2DB`, Welcome) explains claiming and force-loading chunks from the map. | Good default for your first home and machines; keep mining and destructive experiments away from the living area. This is *not* a rule that a base must be in the Overworld. |
| **Mining Dimension outpost or base** | **“Mining Dimension”** (`6D738730B371B152`, Chapter 2: Allthemodium) describes a superflat world, empty surface, ores below, no caves or mob spawns, and recommends a quarry. The chapter also has a task titled **“Visit The Mining Dimension”** (`681CB7DF06F2F506`). | Use its open surface for extraction or an industrial outpost. Making it your whole home is a *preference*, not something the quest tells you to do; the quest frames it as a mining location. |
| **Compact Machines** | **“Compact Machines”** (`0539AF15A10B2859`, Building Tips) says a placed machine leads into a compacted dimension via the Personal Shrinking Device; its walls cannot be broken. | Useful *later* if a bounded interior appeals to you. Treat this as an optional space-management choice, not proof that it can replace every outdoor build or that its contents always run unattended. |

**Chunk-loading caution.** The Welcome **“Claiming Chunks”** description says to open the map with `M`, use *Claimed Chunks*, left-click/drag to claim, and shift-left-click a chunk to force-load it (striped lines indicate success). That documents a control, **not** a promise that every dimension or interior automatically ticks. The Applied Energistics 2 quest entry for **Spatial Anchor** (`2897FA291E5A38D8`) describes force-loading the chunks occupied by a *powered ME network* (excluding subnetworks); it has **no explicit quest title** in this English config, so Spatial Anchor here is a device name from the description, not a quoted quest title. Do not infer that it loads an entire base or a Compact Machine. `config/hyperbox-common.toml` sets `auto_force_hyperbox_chunks = false` for **Hyperbox** interiors; this is a separate config, **not** evidence that Compact Machines behave the same way. Check loading behavior in your own world before relying on unattended farms.

**What to do first (*judgement*):** Pick a convenient Overworld spot you enjoy, claim the chunks you actually use if appropriate for your world/server, and postpone a permanent relocation. When the Chapter 2 quests introduce the Teleport Pad and Mining Dimension, try an extraction outpost there. If you prefer enclosed builds, read the Building Tips “Compact Machines” entry in your own book before committing to that layout. There is no verified best coordinate, biome, machine recipe, chunk allowance, or cross-dimensional network setup in this guide; consult the in-game quest book and JEI for your version.
